Software & Mobile App

The intelligent control center for your QuailEdge ecosystem. Monitor, automate, and manage everything from a single Bangla-enabled Android App.

Complete Control in Your Pocket

Our dedicated Android application connects seamlessly with the ESP32 over Wi-Fi. It gives you real-time visibility into your farm's health, even when you are away from home.

  • 📊

    Live Dashboard

    Track real-time temperature, humidity, food weight, and water levels instantly.

  • ⚙️

    Smart Automation

    Set thresholds for automatic fan, mist, and bulb activation. Or switch to manual control anytime.

  • 🔔

    Instant Notifications

    Get Bangla alerts on your phone if the temperature spikes, water runs out, or food is low.

  • 📹

    Live Camera Feed (Optional)

    Watch your quails live through an integrated ESP32-CAM module right from the app.

Bangla Voice Assistant

How the Data Flow Works

1

Sense

Sensors (DHT22, MQ-135, Load Cell) gather real-time environmental data.

2

Process

ESP32 processes the data locally using Edge-AI logic (no cloud needed).

3

Transmit

Data is sent via Wi-Fi to your Android app using MQTT/HTTP protocols.

4

Control

You monitor live stats and control fans, mist, and pumps directly from the app.
