Weekly Progress

Our team's week-by-week journey building the QuailEdge ecosystem — from literature review to working prototype.

WEEK 1

Research & Planning

Goal: Understand the problem space and define the project scope.

Conducted detailed literature review on urban poultry farming challenges, Edge-AI applications, and IoT-based automation systems. Studied Coturnix japonica (Japanese Quail) biology, housing requirements, and optimal environmental conditions.

Literature Review Problem Definition
WEEK 2

Component Selection

Goal: Choose the right hardware for Edge-AI processing and IoT connectivity.

Selected ESP32 WROOM32 as the central microcontroller due to its dual-core processing and built-in Wi-Fi. Finalized sensors: DHT22 for temperature/humidity, MQ-135 for air quality, HX711 + 5kg load cell for feed monitoring. Prepared the complete Bill of Materials (BOM).

Hardware Selection BOM Preparation
WEEK 3

Circuit Prototyping

Goal: Build and test the first electronic prototype on breadboard.

Assembled the basic circuit connecting ESP32 with DHT22 and MQ-135 sensors. Successfully read temperature, humidity, and air quality data on the serial monitor. Tested 12V cooling fans with IRLZ34N MOSFET drivers.

Breadboard Testing Sensor Calibration
WEEK 4

Software Development

Goal: Write firmware for ESP32 to handle sensor data and automation logic.

Developed the initial C++ firmware in Arduino IDE. Implemented real-time sensor reading, threshold-based fan control, and Wi-Fi connectivity for IoT data transmission. Tested data logging to a local server.

ESP32 Firmware Wi-Fi Setup
WEEK 5

Enclosure Design

Goal: Design the physical micro-poultry structure for urban environments.

Designed the compact ecosystem with sloped mesh floor (5-7° angle) for automatic egg roll-out. Planned natural habitat elements: plants, soil bedding, and hiding areas. Selected 5mm tempered glass for viewing panels and aluminum frame for durability.

CAD Design Material Selection
WEEK 6

Integration & Testing

Goal: Integrate all components into a working system.

Combined sensors, actuators, and cleaning system into the prototype enclosure. Tested automated cleaning conveyor, water pump for mist cooling, and gravity-fed water system. Refined the Edge-AI logic for predictive climate control.

System Integration Edge-AI Logic
WEEK 7

Data Collection & Analysis

Goal: Deploy the system with real quails and collect performance data.

Placed 3-5 quails in the ecosystem for a trial run. Monitored temperature stability, humidity control, air quality, and egg production. Collected 7 days of continuous sensor data for analysis and model refinement.

Live Testing Data Analysis
WEEK 8

Documentation & Finalization

Goal: Complete project documentation, draft paper, and final presentation.

Wrote the complete draft paper covering system architecture, Edge-AI algorithms, results, and future scope. Prepared the project report with detailed schematics and BOM. Finalized the website and presentation materials.

Draft Paper Final Report
