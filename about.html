About QuailEdge

An Edge-AI and IoT-Driven Closed-Loop Precision Micro-Poultry Ecosystem for Organic Japanese Quail (Coturnix japonica) Farming in Dense Urban Environments.

Reimagining Urban Poultry Farming with Intelligence

QuailEdge is a smart, closed-loop ecosystem designed to make organic Japanese Quail (Coturnix japonica) farming possible — even inside the smallest urban apartments. By combining Edge-AI and IoT technologies, we've created a self-sustaining micro-poultry system that manages climate, feeding, cleaning, and egg collection automatically.

Traditional poultry farming demands space, time, and constant attention. Our system eliminates all three barriers. The birds live in a natural habitat with plants, soil, and proper airflow — while sensors, actuators, and an on-board AI handle everything else.

Whether you're a busy professional, a health-conscious family, or an urban farmer at heart — QuailEdge lets you raise your own organic quails without the mess, smell, or hassle.

The Problem We Solve

Urban families want fresh, organic eggs — but traditional poultry farming is impossible in dense cities. Here's why:

📏

No Space

Apartments don't have yards. Traditional coops need significant square footage that urban homes simply cannot provide.

💨

Bad Odor

Bird droppings produce ammonia and strong smells. In a closed apartment, this becomes unbearable within days.

⏰

Time-Consuming

Feeding, watering, cleaning, and egg collection require daily manual effort that busy professionals don't have.

🌡️

Climate Sensitivity

Quails need specific temperature and humidity ranges. Without proper control, they get stressed and stop laying eggs.

🐛

Disease Risk

Poor air quality and dirty environments cause respiratory diseases, reducing flock health and productivity.

🥚

Egg Loss

Eggs laid on bedding get dirty, cracked, or lost. Searching through soil for eggs is frustrating and unhygienic.

Our Solution

QuailEdge solves every one of these problems through intelligent automation and Edge-AI.

🏠

Compact Design

A vertical, apartment-friendly enclosure that fits in a corner — just like a small piece of furniture.

🌿

Natural Habitat

Plants, soil, logs, and natural airflow create a stress-free environment that keeps quails healthy and happy.

🌬️

Carbon-Filtered Air

MQ-135 sensor detects ammonia. Exhaust fan with carbon filter automatically removes odor — keeping your home fresh.

🤖

Full Automation

Feeding, watering, cleaning, and egg collection are all automated. You only interact with the system once a day.

🧠

Edge-AI Control

ESP32 runs AI logic on-device — no cloud dependency. It makes split-second decisions based on real-time sensor data.

📱

Remote Monitoring

Wi-Fi connectivity lets you monitor temperature, humidity, feed level, and water status from your phone — anywhere.

How It Works

From sensor reading to automatic action — all in milliseconds.

1

Sense

DHT22, MQ-135, water level sensor, and load cell continuously monitor the environment.

2

Analyze

ESP32's Edge-AI compares readings against optimal thresholds for quail health.

3

Act

Fans, pumps, servos, and conveyor belts activate automatically to maintain ideal conditions.

4

Notify

Data is sent via Wi-Fi to your phone — with alerts for feed refill, water top-up, or unusual conditions.
