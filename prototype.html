Prototype Design

A complete engineering breakdown of the QuailEdge closed-loop ecosystem — from exploded layer views to top-down zone layouts.

🌿 Natural Environment 🌡️ Smart Climate Control 💧 Humidity Regulation ⚙️ Automatic Cleaning 🥚 Easy Egg Collection

01 Bottom Section — Exploded View

1

Natural Activity Area

The top layer where quails live, play, and rest. Designed to mimic their natural habitat.

  • Soil/Sand Bedding for dust bathing
  • Plants & Logs for hiding and pecking
  • Rocks & Branches for natural enrichment
2

Sloped Mesh Floor

A slanted wire mesh layer that separates birds from waste and rolls eggs forward.

  • 5-7° Angle for gravity-based egg roll
  • 7mm Mesh Openings safe for quail feet
  • Welded Steel for durability & easy cleaning
3

Egg Collection Channel

A smooth, rounded gutter that guides eggs from the mesh floor to the front drawer.

  • Roll-out Design minimizes egg breakage
  • Front Access Drawer for easy collection
  • Smooth Surface prevents cracking
4

Waste Collection Tray

The bottom layer that captures droppings and channels them to a sealed compost-ready tray.

  • Waterproof PP/HDPE material
  • Automatic Scraper + Water Rinse Line
  • Sloped to Drain for complete cleaning

02 Cross Section — Side View

A

Airflow Dynamics

Fresh air enters from the left mesh panel, circulates inside, and exits through the top exhaust fan with carbon filter.

  • Free Air Intake with fine insect mesh
  • Exhaust Fan with carbon filter for odor control
  • Continuous Air Exchange prevents ammonia buildup
B

Climate Control System

DHT22 and MQ-135 sensors monitor conditions and trigger automated responses via ESP32.

  • DHT22 for temperature & humidity
  • MQ-135 for air quality (NH3, CO2)
  • Mist Nozzle for humidity regulation
C

Feeding & Watering

Gravity-fed water tank and servo-controlled automatic feeder ensure continuous supply.

  • Gravity-Fed Water Tank with nipple drinkers
  • Servo Gate Feeder with load cell monitoring
  • Low Maintenance — refill once every few days
D

LED Grow Lighting

Full-spectrum LED mimics natural sunlight to support bird health and plant growth inside the habitat.

  • 15H Light / 9H Dark circadian cycle
  • Sun-Like Spectrum for quail wellbeing
  • Energy Efficient 12V cool-white LED chain

03 Top View Layout — Zone Design

①

Green Zone (Plants + Shade)

A dedicated area with plants, logs, and leafy cover that mimics a quail's natural hiding environment.

  • Hiding Area with logs and plants
  • Natural Shade reduces bird stress
  • Polycarbonate Roof with natural sunlight
②

Feeding Zone

Central activity area with the automatic feeder and enough space for 3-5 quails to feed together.

  • Main Activity Area for 3-5 birds
  • Automatic Feeder (Servo + Load Cell)
  • Water Nipple Drinkers nearby
③

Water Zone

The right side houses the water reservoir, sensors, and the mist/humidity control system.

  • Water Tank (gravity-fed, ~2-3L)
  • Water Level Sensor for refill alerts
  • Mist Nozzle for humidity control
④

Egg Roll-out Zone

The front section where eggs collect in a protective drawer, easy to access without disturbing the birds.

  • Front Egg Collection Drawer
  • No-Break Design for safe transport
  • Quick Access for daily collection
