The Research Gap

After reviewing over 100+ papers, our team identified critical flaws in existing smart poultry systems. Here is exactly where the QuailEdge ecosystem bridges the gap.

🌡️

No Autonomous Temperature Control

Previous systems could only monitor temperature but failed to regulate it. When it got too hot, they couldn't automatically turn on exhaust fans to cool the environment down.

💧

No Humidity Control

Existing projects lacked the ability to maintain optimal humidity levels. Without automatic misting, quails become stressed and egg production drops significantly.

🚰

No Automatic Water Measurement

There was no way to track water levels or consumption automatically. Farmers had to manually check the tanks, risking dehydration for the birds if they forgot.

🌾

No Precise Feeding Measure

It was impossible to measure exactly how much food was dispensed or consumed. This led to either overfeeding (wasting money) or underfeeding (stunting growth).

💩

No Waste Impact Analysis

Previous systems never measured how much waste was accumulating or how the resulting toxic ammonia gas was actively harming the birds' respiratory health.

🧹

Improper & Manual Cleaning

All existing systems lacked an automated sanitation mechanism. Manual cleaning was insufficient, allowing bacteria and diseases to spread rapidly inside the cage.

🙅‍♂️

Total Human Dependency

The biggest flaw: birds could not survive if humans were absent. Without a closed-loop system, a simple power outage or vacation could result in total flock mortality.

How QuailEdge Solves This

QuailEdge is not just a monitoring tool; it is a fully autonomous micro-ecosystem. By combining Edge-AI, localized sensor fusion, and a closed-loop design, we ensure that quails receive the exact care they need—whether a human is present or not. From climate control to cleaning and feeding, it operates completely hands-free.
